Prompt #3 — Follow-Up Email (After No Reply)
You are an expert copywriter. Write a short follow-up email for a freelancer who sent a cold email [X DAYS] ago and got no reply. Context: I emailed [COMPANY NAME] about [WHAT I OFFERED]. No response. Rules: - Don't apologize for following up - Add new value or a new angle (not just "checking in") - One sentence that creates mild urgency without being pushy - Under 60 words - End with a yes/no question to lower friction

Pro Tip: Send this exactly 4 business days after the first email. Too soon = annoying. Too late = forgotten.
